### 2. What one zoom step costs today, 1000 blocks

A step is a new z. The view is a clip rectangle 768 px tall a quarter of the way down the document. Each row is one sample per step. 'new sizes': 40 steps of z *= 1.03 from 0.6, so nearly every step asks the atlas for sizes it has not baked. 'sizes seen': the same 40 values of z again straight after (ImGui may have dropped some of them from the atlas in between).
