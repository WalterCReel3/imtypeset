[from builder-7] math-mlist and mathtext done (filed and condensed by lead from the final report; the
headers src/math_mlist.h and src/mathtext.h and docs/divergences.md `## math-mlist`, `## mathtext`
carry the detail).

math-mlist: src/math_atoms.h, src/math_mlist.{h,cpp}, tests/math_golden.h, tests/test_math_mlist.cpp.
28 tests / 218,251 checks in debug, ASan+UBSan, release. All 2,576 cases of mlist.golden and 2,037 of
formulas.golden match node for node (hlist, hpack result, ship commands, or refusal with numbers and
message) under three font sources: recorded answers only, a native restatement of the reference's
fake fonts, and the port's own FontLibrary.
Mutation testing (39 mutants; goldens killed 33, 2 equivalent) exposed four behaviours no golden
covers, now pinned by hard-coded reference answers in the test: the ceiling in
LARGEST_VARIANT_MUST_COVER; the `<=` tie in _delimiter_box; make_op's single step along the variant
chain; the 7-rule-thickness clearance for \atop in display style. Task `oracle-math-gaps` moves them
into the oracle.
Bounds: MAX_MATH_NESTING = 64 (a judgement about stack use; the reference sets a tower 197 deep);
MAX_MATH_ITEMS = 2^20 counted per path. Host lengths clamped to MAX_DIMEN; MathFonts::init refuses
unusable sizes. The builder helpers (group, delimited, ...) are not ported.
Field is 16 bytes, MathItem 64; callers read field.box() and field.mu().

mathtext: src/mathtext.{h,cpp}, src/mathtext_tables.inc (generated by tools/gen_mathtext_tables.py,
`--check` passes; NOT clang-formatted on purpose), tests/test_mathtext.cpp. 16 tests / 204,404 checks
in debug, ASan+UBSan, release. All 3,765 sources, display off and on, give the reference's list or its
refusal (kind, codepoint position, byte position, reason string byte for byte). 1,971 sourced formula
cases go source -> boxes -> commands equal to the reference, incl. 21 layout refusals. A 30,000-source
fuzz always gets an answer. One golden gap found by mutation (`{x_2}` standing alone), pinned by 11
hard-coded lists.
Decisions: malformed UTF-8 decodes to U+FFFD; a refusal carries codepoint AND byte position; `typeset`
takes the fonts rather than building them; markdown_math and Formula.source are NOT ported (api-math
keeps the source text for copy); when a text resolver does not know the face the reason ends at
"did not resolve". The deepest list parse emits is 49 levels (static_assert + test).
Not verified: `%.2f` rounding on MSVC; worst-case memory before TOO_LARGE.
