1. ImWchar must be 32-bit (D8) - NOT in any task spec. ImGui's default ImWchar is 16 bits; the reference mints synthetic codepoints in plane 15 (fontlib.py SYNTHETIC_FIRST=0xFFFFD downward to 0xF0000) and math alphabets are plane 1. The skeleton's imgui target was 16-bit. I made cmake/imgui.cmake define IMGUI_USE_WCHAR32 PUBLIC (option IMTYPESET_IMGUI_WCHAR32, default ON); smoke.cpp asserts sizeof(ImWchar)==4. OPEN for font-loader / public-api: a host that brings its own 16-bit-ImWchar imgui target cannot get math by this design - compile error, math-less build, or a BMP-PUA minting range has to be decided there. Many ImGui hosts are 16-bit builds, so this affects the adoption story.
